## Why Eglin AFB Properties Need a Careful Approach

The Florida Panhandle's heat and humidity are not just a comfort issue, they affect how quickly biological material penetrates flooring, subfloor, and wall materials. In a region where afternoon humidity regularly sits high for much of the year, porous materials like carpet padding, grout lines, and baseboards absorb and hold what they come into contact with faster than they would in a drier climate. That means timeline matters. The longer a scene goes unaddressed, the more likely it is that flooring or sections of drywall will need to be removed rather than cleaned, which adds cost and time to a situation that is already difficult.

## Our Crime Scene Cleanup Process

We start with containment, sealing off the affected area so nothing spreads to the rest of the property while we assess what materials are involved and whether they can be cleaned or need to be removed. Our OSHA trained team then handles extraction and disposal of contaminated materials according to accepted biohazard handling practices, followed by cleaning and disinfection of all affected surfaces. The final step is deodorization and a walkthrough to confirm the space is safe to re-enter. We document each stage with photos, which supports your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was done.
