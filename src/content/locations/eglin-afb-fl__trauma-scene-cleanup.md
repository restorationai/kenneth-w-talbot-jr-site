**Trauma scene cleanup needed on or near Eglin Air Force Base?** We answer 24/7, and because Eglin is an active military installation, we understand that getting help to a residence inside the gates involves more than just showing up with a truck. Our team handles the cleanup with the same discretion whether the property sits on base housing or in the surrounding community, so the people affected can step back from a scene they shouldn't have to manage alone.

## Why Eglin AFB Calls for a Different Kind of Response

Eglin AFB is not a typical residential service call. Base housing is managed through military channels rather than a standard property management office, and any crew entering the installation has to clear Security Forces access control before reaching the address. That adds a coordination step most restoration companies in the Panhandle never have to think about. We plan for it before we leave Freeport, confirming who the point of contact is, whether it's a housing office representative, a commanding officer's staff, or a family member, so the visit happens without delay once we're cleared through the gate.

## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process

The work itself follows the same careful sequence everywhere we go, but the sequencing around a base property shifts slightly. Once on site, our technicians isolate the affected area, remove and dispose of materials that cannot be safely cleaned, and treat surfaces and sub-surfaces according to recognized biohazard decontamination standards. We work in OSHA Trained protective equipment throughout, and we document the work as we go, both for the family's peace of mind and for any claim that follows. Off-base properties in Eglin's surrounding neighborhoods are typically quicker to access, but the cleaning and sanitization standard doesn't change based on address.

## Insurance, Privacy, and Coordination

Families dealing with a trauma scene are often also dealing with a chain of phone calls: housing office, insurance adjuster, sometimes a military liaison. We try to reduce that load where we can. Our Florida DBPR license backs our work, and we provide the documentation carriers typically ask for. On base, we coordinate directly with whoever manages the housing unit rather than asking a grieving family to be the go-between, and we keep the number of people who know the details of a visit as small as possible.

## Local note

Because Eglin is an active duty installation, every technician and vehicle we send onto the base has to be cleared through Security Forces before work can begin, which means the scheduling call matters as much as the cleanup itself. We've learned to ask for the escort or access instructions up front so the crew isn't sitting at a gate waiting on paperwork while a family waits on the other end.
