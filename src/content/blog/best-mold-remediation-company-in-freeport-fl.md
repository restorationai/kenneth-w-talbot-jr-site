## How To Compare Mold Remediation Companies in Freeport

Choosing a mold remediation contractor is not the same as hiring a general handyman. The [IICRC S520 Standard for Professional Mold Remediation](https://www.iicrc.org) defines the protocols that separate a legitimate remediation from a surface treatment that leaves the problem in place. When you are evaluating any company, ask these questions directly:

1. **Do you use physical containment?** Poly sheeting and negative air pressure prevent cross-contamination during removal. If a company skips containment, spores spread to clean areas.
2. **Do you use HEPA filtration?** Air scrubbers with HEPA filters capture spores down to 0.3 microns. Standard shop vacs do not.
3. **Do you remove affected materials or just treat them?** Mold-saturated drywall and insulation generally need to come out. Encapsulation alone is not remediation.
4. **Do you provide post-remediation verification?** A clearance test after work is complete confirms the job is done. Some companies skip this step.
5. **Are you licensed and insured in Florida?** Ask for the license number. Verify it with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation if you want to be thorough.
