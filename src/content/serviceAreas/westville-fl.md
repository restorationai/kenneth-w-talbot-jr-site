## Restoration emergencies common in Westville

Westville sits in the north Florida panhandle, where long, humid summers and an active hurricane season (June through November) create two very different but related problems. Heavy rain events can overwhelm drainage faster than sandy soil can absorb it, leaving crawlspaces and low-lying yards holding water for days. That same humidity, combined with any undetected leak, gives mold a head start, visible colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours on wet drywall or framing. Wind-driven storms also bring the more sudden emergencies: downed limbs through roofs, shingles stripped by gusts, and water intrusion through compromised flashing that isn't caught until a ceiling stain shows up days later.

## Services we provide in Westville

**Mold remediation** addresses the humidity-driven growth that follows an unresolved leak or storm intrusion, with containment and remediation scoped to the affected materials rather than a blanket demo.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Westville

Much of Westville's housing stock reflects its rural panhandle roots: older single-story frame homes on crawlspaces or piers, mixed with newer slab-on-grade construction and a notable share of manufactured and mobile homes. Older framing may still carry galvanized supply lines or original cast iron drains, both of which are past their expected service life and prone to pinhole leaks or backups that aren't discovered until water has already soaked subflooring or insulation.

Regional soils are predominantly sandy, which drains faster than clay but doesn't stop water from pooling in low crawlspaces or against foundations during heavy rain.
